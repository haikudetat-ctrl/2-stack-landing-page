# Privacy policy publication review

The proposed /privacy page covers 2Stack's own services, compensated referrals, and any SMS programs it operates. It does not implement texting, consent capture, STOP/HELP handling, tracking controls, or privacy-request processing. Review the following against actual business practices before merging and publishing:

- Confirm the legal operator behind 2Stack, the scope of its branded sites, and that team@2stackops.com is monitored for privacy and messaging requests. Add the legal entity and mailing address if required for the jurisdictions served.
- Confirm all mobile numbers, SMS subscriber lists, and opt-in evidence are excluded from affiliate or third-party marketing disclosures. A requested introduction must not transfer a mobile number contrary to this promise. If actual practices differ, change the practices or obtain a legally reviewed policy reflecting them; do not publish inaccurate promises.
- Inventory actual hosting, analytics, embedded-media, referral, CRM/webhook, messaging, booking, and payment providers. Verify cookies and any consent or applicable sale/sharing opt-out controls, including recognition of legally required preference signals. Verify contractual restrictions, security safeguards, retention, deletion, international-transfer protections, and request/appeal handling.
- Keep optional marketing SMS consent separate from the privacy policy, terms acceptance, and purchase. Identify the actual sender and message categories; disclose frequency, rates, automation, STOP/HELP, and link the privacy policy and program terms at enrollment. Retain evidence of the consent wording, date, and source. Obtain independent consent for another business; consent to 2Stack does not transfer.
- Implement and test STOP, HELP, reasonable alternative revocation methods, suppression across connected systems, and non-promotional opt-out confirmation before operating a messaging program. Use the current applicable legal and provider deadlines.
- Add program-specific SMS terms before enrollment launches. Do not claim carrier registration, legal compliance, or provider approval solely because this page exists.
- Confirm geographic scope and any jurisdiction-specific notices or rights required for the business. Have qualified counsel review the policy and actual affiliate/referral model before publication.
